Pick Your Travel Spot
📋 How this works: Imagine you're planning a day trip to a famous destination in India. You open a travel app and see two specific places to visit — like a monument, a natural site, or a cultural spot. Each place comes with some quick details to help you decide.

Look at both options and pick the one you'd actually want to visit. If you don't like either one, you can say "neither."

One thing to keep in mind: Please choose as if you're really going. Just go with what feels right for you!
